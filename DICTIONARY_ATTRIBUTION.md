# Third-party dictionary attribution

ORACLE includes a compact, reformatted dictionary index generated from **Open English WordNet 2025+**.

- Project: Open English WordNet team
- Source repository: https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet
- Release: https://github.com/globalwordnet/english-wordnet/releases/tag/2025-edition
- Dataset: `english-wordnet-2025-plus-json.zip`
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
- Underlying resource: Princeton WordNet 3.1

Open English WordNet is derived from Princeton WordNet and further developed by the Open English WordNet team. The dataset is released under CC BY 4.0. Attribution is required to both Princeton WordNet and the Open English WordNet team. ORACLE reformats its lexical forms, definitions and example sentences into a compressed lookup index. This is not Cambridge Dictionary material.
